# Research Watch — 2026-10-04

## SHARPO: Segment-Level Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00838
- Submitted 2026-09-30; newly surfaced in the current tracker sweep.
- Core idea: keep the GRPO trajectory advantage, but use a teacher–student log-probability gap to compute a bounded segment-level multiplier shared by all tokens in an environment-facing segment.
- Why it matters: this is a strong new baseline for the design principle “preserve global task credit, redistribute/reweight where it lands” rather than replacing the global objective.
- Relation to Adaptive Harness / H-PAIR: generic segment-level credit is now more crowded. H-PAIR should emphasize same-state semantic Harness interventions and routing credit. SHARPO is especially relevant because it tests on ALFWorld and WebShop, matching the planned benchmark family.

## ActiveSaddler: Automated Curriculum Learning for Agent Harness Optimization
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00906
- Submitted 2026-10-01; absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: co-evolve the training curriculum with the Harness. Recurring failures become non-stationary bandit arms, and the system prioritizes scenarios by estimated future learning progress while continuing to discover new failure patterns.
- Reported result: +4.4 Pass@1 on GAIA2 and +7.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.0 over the same Harness optimizer with a fixed scenario order.
- Why it matters: adaptive Harness optimization now includes adaptive data/scenario allocation, not only Harness modification.
- Relation to our direction: do not frame adaptive branch/sample allocation itself as the central novelty. H-PAIR remains distinct if it learns policy-controlled use/skip at a state and assigns counterfactual routing credit; ActiveSaddler can instead motivate future learning-progress-aware branch budgeting.

## AIM: Agentic Idea Management for Automated Research
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38445
- Submitted 2026-09-29; previously missed and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: explicitly manage idea-driven scientific search using an Agentic Surrogate and Agentic Acquisition mechanism, audit idea–implementation alignment, and allocate experimental budget over parallel idea branches.
- Reported result: on 10 AutoLab tasks, +1.6 pp on system optimization and +4.9 pp on long-horizon model/CUDA tasks over the strongest baseline; up to 3.1x faster to reach the best baseline result.
- Why it matters: it directly occupies the “idea set/tree management + branch selection + research budget allocation” space and provides theory arguing that explicit semantic coverage becomes more valuable when good directions are sparse.
- Relation to Creative Agent / idea graphs: a publishable Idea Graph contribution now needs more than organizing and selecting branches. Stronger differentiators are relational structure (support/conflict/dependency), evidence provenance, graph-structured credit, or learned graph operations. Its semantic-coverage analysis is also directly relevant to diversity-preserving ideation.

## Explicit Trajectory Diversity for RL-Based Post-Training of LLM Agents
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38805
- Submitted 2026-09-30; absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: define diversity with user-specified task-specific trajectory descriptors and optimize a set-level diversity objective inside group-based policy optimization via Trajectory-guided Joint Policy Optimization (TJPO).
- Why it matters: it replaces vague stochastic “diversity” with interpretable behavioral dimensions and reports distinct successful strategies while retaining competitive task performance on Sokoban and ALFWorld.
- Relation to our direction: diversity-preserving RL should likely operate over semantic descriptors rather than token entropy. For scientific ideation, descriptors could encode hypothesis family, mechanism, evidence source, or experimental strategy; for Harnessed Agentic RL, they could prevent premature collapse to one use/skip pattern, but diversity should remain subordinate to utility rather than become an end in itself.

## Synthesis
Two boundaries moved this round. First, generic process credit is getting stronger while explicitly preserving global GRPO-style task signal (SHARPO), which reinforces the case for keeping H-PAIR's novelty on semantic Harness interventions rather than generic fine-grained credit. Second, both Harness optimization and scientific ideation are becoming allocation problems over evolving targets or semantic branches (ActiveSaddler, AIM), while TJPO shows that diversity can be defined directly over interpretable trajectory semantics rather than sampling noise.
