# Literature Update — 2026-09-15

Fresh arXiv items that materially affect Harnessed Agentic RL, scientific research agents, skill evolution, or adaptive scaffolding.

## Stellar Colosseum: A Many-Agent Harness for Long-Horizon Research in Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15983
- Submitted: 2026-09-14
- Core idea: model-agnostic research harness for long-horizon mathematical/TCS research. It explores alternative strategies before proof construction, uses a readiness gate before decomposition, represents proof plans as interdependent section-level subproblems, and routes verifier findings back to affected sections.
- Why it matters: a concrete example of a heterogeneous research harness in which strategy exploration, gating, decomposition, falsification, verification, and aggregation are all explicit external procedures. Useful as a realistic reference for the harness action space beyond simple skill injection or reflection.
- Relation to our direction: strengthens the case that realistic harnesses have multiple intervention types and control-flow decisions; adaptive RL over such interventions remains distinct from merely designing a stronger fixed research workflow.

## AlgoEvo: Self-Evolving Agentic Search for Automated Algorithm Discovery
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15820
- Submitted: 2026-09-14
- Core idea: replaces rigid algorithm-discovery pipelines with an interactive agent that diagnoses and edits code from execution feedback, a design-skill hub, and hierarchical experience organized as task-level search trees plus cross-task reusable skills.
- Why it matters: very close to creative/scientific search. It shows self-evolution through external skill/experience structures, while preserving a general core discovery engine.
- Relation to our direction: another collision against generic "self-evolving harness/skills" novelty. The more defensible gap is how to assign credit across heterogeneous interventions, decide when a component is useful, and adapt the harness jointly with policy learning.

## SkillLift: Learning Dense Rubrics from Sparse Oracles for Efficient Skill Evolution
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15396
- Submitted: 2026-09-14
- Core idea: oracle rollouts are expensive, so SkillLift learns an oracle-aligned rubric as a cheap structured surrogate. Inner-loop skill search uses the frozen rubric; occasional outer-loop oracle rollouts re-align it through ranking supervision.
- Reported effect: 40–70% lower token cost than frontier evolving-skill methods while improving skill evolution.
- Why it matters: directly addresses the compute bottleneck in self-evolving harness/skill systems. It suggests that expensive counterfactual/oracle rollouts can be amortized by a learned surrogate rather than used at every candidate update.
- Relation to our direction: relevant if adaptive Harness intervention requires costly with/without-intervention rollouts; a cheap gate/rubric could select where expensive causal tests are worth spending.

## RSIAgent: Autonomous Exploration for Recursive Self-improvement in New Environments
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15364
- Submitted: 2026-09-14
- Core idea: training-free recursive self-improvement via a curriculum/actor/verifier system that autonomously explores a new environment and writes reusable causal action-condition-consequence knowledge into memory. Uses broad-then-deep exploration to cover diverse structures first, then hard cases and hidden constraints.
- Why it matters: shows a strong self-evolution route entirely through external memory/harness state, with no parameter updates.
- Relation to our direction: useful contrast to Agentic RL. If external memory can absorb environment-specific knowledge cheaply, our RL contribution needs to explain what should be learned in weights versus retained/adapted in the harness.

## Not All Prompts Are Equal: Exploration-Guided Prompt Scaffolding for Multimodal Reinforcement Post-Training
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15051
- Submitted: 2026-09-14; EMNLP 2026 Main
- Core idea: prompts differ in learning value for the current policy. An Exploration Potential Score (EPS), computed from on-policy rollout statistics, estimates prompt utility. Low-utility prompts are scaffolded/re-written by a teacher to make subsequent RL more informative rather than simply discarded.
- Why it matters: a clean example of training-time scaffolding that adapts to current policy state using cheap rollout statistics.
- Relation to our direction: strongly aligned with the principle "adapt the scaffold to the learner." It operates on task prompts rather than runtime harness components, but the same policy-dependent utility idea could be generalized to Harness intervention selection.

## G2RPO-A: Guided Group Relative Policy Optimization with Adaptive Guidance
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13023
- Venue/status: ACL 2026 Main Conference
- Code: https://github.com/T-Lab-CUHKSZ/G2RPO-A
- Core idea: split each GRPO group into guided and unguided rollouts. Guided rollouts receive ground-truth reasoning-prefix tokens, and the guidance length is adjusted online according to the ratio between the current batch reward and recent historical rewards.
- Adaptive rule: when recent rewards improve, guidance length is reduced to make future samples harder; when rewards fall, guidance length is increased to make them easier. The paper therefore adapts intervention strength to the current training state rather than using a fixed or hand-written decay schedule.
- Why it matters: this is a direct prior for the general principle "external assistance should depend on policy competence." It is closer to adaptive curriculum/scaffolding than to full runtime Harness control, because the intervention type is fixed (ground-truth reasoning guidance) and adaptation acts mainly on global guidance length/ratio rather than choosing heterogeneous Harness components at state-level decision points.
- Relation to our direction: important novelty collision against any claim phrased only as "dynamically reduce/increase guidance during RL." Our stronger target must operate at finer granularity: where to intervene, which Harness component to use, and how to credit heterogeneous interventions conditioned on trajectory state.

## Takeaway
The strongest new signal is not another GRPO variant. It is that adaptive external structures are becoming increasingly explicit:

1. realistic research harnesses contain many heterogeneous operations (Stellar Colosseum),
2. self-evolution often accumulates skills/experience outside model weights (AlgoEvo, RSIAgent),
3. expensive oracle/counterfactual supervision is being amortized through learned surrogates (SkillLift),
4. scaffolding is increasingly conditioned on the current policy's learning state (Exploration-Guided Prompt Scaffolding), and
5. G2RPO-A already demonstrates policy-state-dependent adjustment of assistance strength inside GRPO.

For our direction, this reinforces a sharper target: **policy-dependent selection and credit assignment over heterogeneous Harness interventions**, rather than generic skill evolution, fixed workflow optimization, or merely adapting the scalar strength of one guidance mechanism.
