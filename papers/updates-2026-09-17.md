# Literature Update — 2026-09-17

Only papers/developments that materially affect the current Adaptive Harness / Harnessed Agentic RL direction are included. De-duplicated against `papers/papers.csv`, `papers/recent-and-adjacent.md`, and the 2026-09-16 update.

## ScienceBuddy: Recursive-in-Recursive Self-Improvement for Interactive Scientific Agents
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Code: https://github.com/Gen-Verse/ScienceBuddy
- Core idea: couples two improvement loops: an inner loop evolves the harness with model weights fixed, while an outer loop trains the model with reinforcement learning under the improved harness. Researcher requests, feedback, and execution evidence are converted into reusable tasks and evaluation rubrics.
- Why it matters: this is a direct prior/collision for the broad claim that harness evolution and model RL should co-adapt. It is especially relevant because the target domain is scientific work rather than a generic agent benchmark.
- Relation to our direction: our novelty cannot be merely “harness improves, then model trains under it.” The stronger remaining question is finer-grained **state-/training-signal-conditioned Harness intervention, component-specific credit, and explicit measurement of what becomes internalized vs what should remain external**. ScienceBuddy also suggests using real interaction traces as a source of adaptive Harness supervision.
- Caveat: the paper currently emphasizes the recursive architecture and case studies more than a clean causal analysis of heterogeneous Harness components or a general internalization criterion.

## Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858
- Date: 2026-09-14
- Project: https://dream-rsi.com/
- Core idea: keeps the underlying discovery agent fixed and improves an **external exploration/orchestration policy**. Completed discovery trees become replay simulators; many alternative exploration policies are evaluated cheaply off-policy, then the best policy is redeployed online.
- Why it matters: this is strong evidence for the “Harness always exists but its configuration/policy changes over time” formulation. The dynamic object is not necessarily an on/off component trigger; it can be the exploration budget, branching pattern, concurrency, continuation rule, and stopping rule of the Harness itself.
- Relation to our direction: directly motivates separating **model learning** from **Harness allocation/evolution**. It also provides a possible answer to expensive counterfactual Harness probing: reuse historical branch trees as a replay world instead of re-running every intervention online.
- Diversity implication: the project reports that semantic guidance from prior trajectories can over-constrain search and suppress diverse exploration, while replay-based policy improvement performs better under matched budgets. This is directly relevant to diversity-preserving idea search and argues against collapsing experience into one “best direction” prompt.
- Caveat: its reported domains are algorithm engineering, mathematical optimization, and GPU kernels; the exploration policy is externally evolved rather than learned as part of the base LLM policy.

## Stellar Colosseum: A Many-Agent Harness for Long-Horizon Research in Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15983
- Date: 2026-09-14
- Core idea: a model-agnostic research Harness that explores multiple strategies, uses a readiness gate before decomposition, represents proof plans as dependency-aware subproblems, routes verifier findings back to the affected section, preserves failed approaches/objections, and aggregates parallel candidates through a tree structure.
- Why it matters: this is one of the clearest recent examples of a Harness for **research rather than ordinary task completion**. It operationalizes several structures we care about for idea generation: explicit graph/dependency state, local repair rather than whole-trajectory restart, parallel diversity, falsification, and persistent negative evidence.
- Relation to our direction: suggests that the Harness state should preserve **where a weakness belongs** rather than reduce everything to one scalar reward. For Adaptive Harnessed RL, verifier feedback could be attached to the affected plan/idea node and used as localized process-level credit or as a trigger for selective intervention. It also gives a concrete research-Harness baseline for future idea-generation experiments.
- Caveat: the current contribution is primarily a fixed multi-agent inference Harness; it does not itself train the base model with RL or study model-side internalization.

## Takeaway
The most consequential new pressure on our framing is **ScienceBuddy**: broad harness-model co-evolution is now clearly occupied. A stronger formulation for our project should therefore center on (1) dynamic allocation/configuration of heterogeneous Harness components, (2) intervention-specific or structure-preserving credit, (3) explicit internalization/externalization analysis, and (4) diversity-aware exploration. Dream-RSI supplies a concrete mechanism for evolving the external orchestration layer from replayable histories, while Stellar Colosseum supplies a concrete research-Harness design in which dependency structure and localized verifier feedback are first-class objects.
