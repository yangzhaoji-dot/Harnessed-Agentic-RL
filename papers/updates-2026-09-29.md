# Research Watch — 2026-09-29

## RINI: Seeing the Prior Is Not Enough
- arXiv 2609.33284.
- New and absent from the tracker.
- Audits research-proposal contribution claims against prior work, checks the remaining distinction, and locally revises unsupported novelty claims.
- Relevance: idea generation needs evidence attached to contribution claims or idea-graph nodes, not only proposal-level novelty scores. This can be an optional literature-inspection Harness near uncertain novelty boundaries.

## Surprising Success, Repeated Failure
- arXiv 2609.33781; method: EAPO.
- New and absent from the tracker.
- Redistributes response-level advantage across tokens using policy entropy and advantage sign: reinforce high-entropy success, penalize low-entropy failure, and attenuate penalties at uncertain failure points.
- Relevance: a strong diversity-preserving credit baseline. It supports preserving global GRPO credit while changing where credit lands, rather than adding an unrelated local reward.

## Learning What to Skip
- arXiv 2609.30734.
- New and absent from the tracker.
- Uses controlled skip interventions to estimate the conditional utility of planning, verification, summarization, and other workflow components.
- Relevance: very close to Adaptive Harness. It frames optional component use as counterfactual credit assignment and should be treated as a direct novelty boundary and baseline for use-vs-skip interventions.

## Agentic Multi-Turn Reasoning: A Fairness Approach
- arXiv 2609.33323; accepted to NeurIPS 2026.
- New and absent from the tracker.
- Fair-MPO combines multi-level preference optimization for long-horizon agentic reasoning with an objective that reduces dominance by frequent patterns.
- Relevance: rare but informative strategies can otherwise be washed out by dominant trajectory modes; this is complementary to entropy-based diversity preservation.
