# Research Watch — 2026-09-27

## DeepInstructor: An Agentic AI Instructor for Experience-Driven Idea Evaluation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22104
- Status: newly surfaced in the recent arXiv announcement stream; not previously present in this repository's tracker.
- Core idea: build an Experience Graph from 58,607 peer reviews and use a ReAct-style evaluator to retrieve dimension-specific evidence for novelty, significance, and feasibility judgments.
- Reported result: Hit@1 and Hit@2 alignment with human judgments improve by 24.4% and 29.7% over the compared baselines.
- Why it matters: the bottleneck in automated science is increasingly idea evaluation rather than raw idea generation. DeepInstructor turns reviewer experience into structured, retrievable evidence instead of relying only on an LLM judge's parametric knowledge.
- Relation to Adaptive Harness / Harnessed Agentic RL: the Experience Graph is a natural external Harness component for research agents. A useful next question is not only whether to retrieve review experience, but when that external evaluation evidence has enough marginal value to justify invocation, and whether repeated evaluator guidance can later be internalized into the policy without collapsing idea diversity.
- Relation to idea graphs: this is complementary to graphs that represent the evolving idea itself. One graph can encode the proposal state; a separate experience graph can supply evidence-grounded evaluation and repair signals.
