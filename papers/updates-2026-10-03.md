# Research Watch — 2026-10-03

## Harness Annealing: Learning to Act with Less External Control
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01235
- New on 2026-10-01 and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: train models on successful trajectories collected under progressively weaker harnesses, with explicit control supervision, so the model gradually assumes control decisions previously supplied by the harness.
- Why it matters: this is a direct internalization/externalization prior. It gives explicit checkpoint-wise evaluation under multiple deployment harnesses rather than inferring internalization from declining call rate.
- Relation to Adaptive Harness / Harnessed Agentic RL: simple “anneal the harness and see whether the model still works” is now occupied. A stronger contribution must identify which capability has transferred, estimate when external support remains marginally useful, or learn a state-dependent model–harness boundary rather than use a global weakening curriculum.

## Not All Experience Belongs in the Weights: Component Routing for Self-Improving GUI Agents
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01787
- New on 2026-10-01 and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: decompose experience into locators, procedures, state facts, and lessons, then route each component either to model weights or to runtime context; recurrence and state-conditionality predict the better destination.
- Why it matters: this is unusually direct evidence that internalization should be component-selective rather than trajectory-level.
- Relation to our direction: it sharpens the model–harness boundary into a routing problem over information/capability types. Adaptive Harness work should distinguish persistent, recurrent knowledge that belongs in weights from state-conditional information that should remain external.

## Causal Memory Policy: Making Memory Utility Identifiable by Intervening on Retrieval
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02070
- New on 2026-10-01 and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: intervene on retrieval itself with known propensities so utility can be estimated even for memories that the current retrieval policy would never expose; uses inverse-propensity weighting to recover causal utility.
- Why it matters: it identifies a positivity/coverage failure that ordinary memory-utility estimators miss.
- Relation to Adaptive Harness / H-PAIR: paired use/skip comparisons are only informative when both routes receive enough support. This is a concrete warning that counterfactual Harness credit can become unidentified if routing collapses; forced exploration and explicit behavior-policy correction are not just engineering conveniences.

## My FAULT: Self-Diagnosis as Credit Assignment in Self-Evolving Agentic Reinforcement Learning
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01161
- New on 2026-10-01 and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: convert natural-language self-diagnosis into terminal-outcome-anchored step credit; validate diagnosis evidence and learn relative error costs online while the policy and diagnoser co-evolve.
- Reported result: 95% signal coverage on ALFWorld versus 41% for GRPO and 72% for GiGPO.
- Why it matters: a strong new process-credit baseline for long-horizon Agentic RL, especially same-outcome rollout groups where vanilla GRPO has no signal.
- Relation to our direction: “finer credit than trajectory GRPO” is increasingly crowded. H-PAIR should keep its claim on semantic Harness intervention and route-specific counterfactual credit rather than generic step-level redistribution.

## ScholarCatalyst: A Benchmark for Retrieving Papers That Inspire New Research
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02202
- New on 2026-10-01 and absent from the tracker.
- Core idea: 184 lead authors of 207 recent CS papers label which prior papers would have advanced their project, yielding a benchmark for retrieving genuinely catalytic literature from only the corpus available when the project began.
- Key result: agentic search underperforms embedding retrieval in Recall@20 (0.42 vs 0.48); even a strong agent reaches only 0.51.
- Why it matters: this isolates a core bottleneck in scientific ideation that current research agents still handle poorly: finding the prior work that changes what ideas become thinkable, not merely retrieving topically similar papers.
- Relation to Creative Agent / idea graphs: inspiration retrieval should be treated as a distinct action/objective from relevance retrieval. An idea graph could explicitly attach catalytic papers to branches and reward retrieval by downstream idea transformation, novelty, or feasibility rather than semantic similarity alone.

## Synthesis
The most consequential boundary shift this round is that **internalization is becoming an explicit routing problem**: what belongs in weights, what stays in external state, and when control can safely move from the harness to the policy. In parallel, Agentic RL work is making process credit denser, so H-PAIR's defensible core is increasingly the **semantic use/skip intervention at a Harness boundary**, with adequate route coverage and behavior correction. For Creative Agent work, ScholarCatalyst adds a concrete benchmark for one upstream source of idea quality: retrieving research that is genuinely catalytic rather than merely relevant.
